Project name: Fluuix Treasury Box

Description:
Fluuix Treasury Box helps a Latin American merchant keep enough local-currency stablecoin for supplier payments and see what is left to rotate. On Celo mainnet the merchant's home balance is a Ripio wFIAT token, starting with wARS. The app lists that balance, reserves scheduled payments for a set number of days, and blocks a rotation that would spend the reserve. A daily review states the reserve and the amount that could move. Any live transfer or swap asks for a wallet signature. Rotation quotes use Textile on Celo; without an executable quote the app does not swap. Ripio on-ramp and off-ramp are links, not completed ramps. This entry is the Celo and Ripio wFIAT flow.

Narrative:
At the end of the day the merchant must pay suppliers in pesos. Holding the whole till in wARS is simple, and moving the surplus into another stablecoin is a treasury choice with price and fee risk. The product makes the cash floor visible and refuses a move that breaks it. It does not promise that the surplus will be worth more in the morning.
